Meet the doctor

Modern technique, human care.

Dr. Jorge Latoni is certified by the American Board of Plastic Surgery and the American Board of Otolaryngology. He is a member of the American Society of Plastic Surgeons and a Fellow of the American College of Surgeons.

With more than 18 years of dedicated and ethical practice, Dr. Latoni has distinguished himself by offering the most modern cosmetic plastic surgery techniques and high impact aesthetic procedures. His specialties include breast augmentation, lift and reduction; abdominoplasty; liposuction; facelifts; and nose, ear and eyelid surgery. A highly trained, certified and experienced professional, he treats every patient with empathy and care.
