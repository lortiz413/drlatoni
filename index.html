Noninvasive body sculpting

truSculpt

A new figure in about three months for men and women, without needles, bruising, incisions or surgery.

The technology works beneath the surface, so it doesn't affect the outer layers of skin or leave marks or discoloration. It is ideal for people who have tried other treatments, or who go to the gym but can't lose fat in specific areas like the waist. Our staff, trained by Cutera, gently glides a handpiece over the area and applies heat. Results start to show in just six weeks.

  1. Heat reaches below the skinEnergy warms the fat cells under the surface.
  2. Fat cells are destroyedThe temperature breaks down the targeted fat.
  3. Your body clears themThe lymphatic system naturally eliminates them.
  4. Gone for goodTreated fat cells are permanently removed.

SmartGraft®, only in Puerto Rico

Hair restoration

Your natural hairline, restored here.

You no longer need to leave the Island for natural looking, permanent hair restoration. Dr. Latoni's Mayagüez office is the sole provider in Puerto Rico of SmartGraft®, combining technology and precision artistry to restore your hairline permanently, without a linear scar.

Before the procedure, a specialist evaluates you to confirm you're a good candidate. On the day, a hairline is designed to harmonize with your facial anatomy, then follicles from a donor area, usually the back of the head, are placed carefully and strategically. The FUE procedure is completed in one day under local anesthesia.
